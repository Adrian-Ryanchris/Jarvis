Jarvis

A personal voice assistant that runs on one person's Mac.

Jarvis is a private desktop application built and used by a single person for their own daily organisation. It is not a public service, has no accounts, and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Answers spoken and typed commands, using AI language models.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail, so it can answer questions such as "what is on today?" or "any new email from Dana?".
  • Adds, moves or deletes calendar events, and saves or sends email drafts, only after the owner approves each change.
  • Keeps to-dos and reminders and a local notes index on the owner's Mac.

Google data

Jarvis asks Google for three separate permissions: calendar events, reading email, and drafting and sending email. It cannot delete or archive email, and it does not access any other Google service. How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
